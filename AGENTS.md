# Project Guidelines & Data Authenticity Rules

## 📌 Core Rule: Video Lecture Content + Government & Academic Authenticity

For all current and future content creation, notes generation, question additions, and syllabus updates in this repository (`cce_mains_revision`), the following two-pillar methodology MUST strictly be followed:

---

### 🎥 Pillar 1: Comprehensive Data Extraction from Video Links
Whenever a video lecture link (YouTube, classroom lecture, etc.) is provided by the user:
1. **Full Lecture Coverage:** Extract and document **100% of the content, concepts, faculty explanations, diagrams, shortcuts/tricks, tables, and exam tips** discussed by the teacher in the video.
2. **Video Metadata Header:** Every note MUST include a dedicated header section with:
   - **શ્રેણી / ચેનલ (Series / Channel):** e.g., Teaching Kishan, Bhoraniya's Guidance, LearningPocket.
   - **લેક્ચર ક્રમાંક & શીર્ષક (Lecture No. & Title)**
   - **માર્ગદર્શક / શિક્ષક (Faculty Name)**
   - **સમયગાળો (Duration):** Exact minutes/seconds.
   - **સત્તાવાર YouTube લિંક (Direct Video Link)**
3. **Didactic Elements:** Retain faculty-provided mnemonics, pedagogical charts, memory tricks (દા.ત. 'ક-પા-મ-ગાં-સા-અ'), and exam-oriented problem-solving approaches.

---

### 🏛️ Pillar 2: Cross-Verification & Enrichment with Authentic Government Sources
All factual data, figures, dates, names, geographic coordinates, administrative facts, and constitutional articles extracted from the video **MUST be cross-verified, authenticated, and enriched against authentic Government of Gujarat, Government of India, GCERT, NCERT, or University Granth Nirman Board sources.**

#### Mandated Authentic Reference Sources:

1. **GCERT & NCERT:**
   - **GCERT (Gujarat Council of Educational Research and Training):** Social Science (Std 6 to 10), History (Std 11 & 12), Geography (Std 11 & 12), Political Science (Std 11 & 12).
   - **NCERT (National Council of Educational Research and Training):** History (*Themes in Indian History* Part 1-3), Geography (*Fundamentals of Physical Geography*, *India: Physical Environment*), Political Science (*Indian Constitution at Work*), Sociology & Psychology (Attitude, Values, Socialization).

2. **University Granth Nirman Board (ગુજરાત યુનિવર્સિટી ગ્રંથ નિર્માણ બોર્ડ):**
   - *ગુજરાતનો રાજકીય અને સાંસ્કૃતિક ઇતિહાસ* (ડૉ. હરિપ્રસાદ શાસ્ત્રી, પ્રવીણચંદ્ર પરીખ)
   - *ગુજરાતની ભૂગોળ* (ડૉ. મંજુલાબેન દવે / આર. કે. રાય)
   - *જાહેર વહીવટ* (ડૉ. હેમંત શાહ)
   - *નીતિશાસ્ત્ર અને મૂલ્યો*

3. **Government of Gujarat Official Portals & Publications:**
   - Gujarat State Portal (`gujaratindia.gov.in`)
   - Gujarat Information Department (`gujaratinformation.gujarat.gov.in`, ગુજરાત પાક્ષિક)
   - Gujarat State Gazetteers (જિલ્લા ગેઝેટિયર્સ)
   - Gujarat Maritime Board (GMB), Forest & Environment Department, Revenue Department, Panchayats & Rural Housing Department.

4. **Government of India Official Portals & Documents:**
   - **The Constitution of India (Bare Act):** Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice (`legislative.gov.in`).
   - **Administrative Reforms:** 2nd Administrative Reforms Commission (2nd ARC) Reports (especially 4th Report *"Ethics in Governance"* and 10th Report *"Personnel Administration"*).
   - **Nolan Committee (1995):** Seven Principles of Public Life.
   - **Census of India & Survey of India:** Official area, boundary, population figures.
   - **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI):** Indus Valley / Harappan excavations, rock edicts, and monument inscriptions.
   - **Geographical Indications Registry (Intellectual Property India):** GI Tags (e.g., Bhalia Wheat, Patan Patola, Gir Kesar Mango).
   - **National Bureau of Animal Genetic Resources (NBAGR / ICAR):** Recognized indigenous livestock breeds (e.g., Banni Buffalo, Kankrej Cow, Vadhiyari Buffalo).

---

## 🚫 Prohibited Practices & Quality Safeguards:
- **No Missed Video Content:** Do not skip or summarize away important lecture points, examples, or flowcharts presented in the video.
- **Correction of Verbal Slips:** If a video lecture contains an accidental slip of the tongue or outdated figure, correct it in the notes while providing both the explanation and the authentic GCERT/Govt citation.
- **Strict Disambiguation:** When exams or older books have alternate names or disputes (e.g., Charotar bounded by Shedhi vs Vatrak, or minor area variations), explicitly mention both the GCERT standard and the official gazetteer note so that candidates are protected in any question format.
- **Accurate Gujarati Terminology:** Retain authentic Gujarati administrative and geographic terminology (e.g., 'સુવર્ણ પર્ણની ભૂમિ', 'રેગુર / કાળી જમીન', 'સુદર્શન તળાવ - પુષ્યગુપ્ત વૈશ્ય', 'આદર્શમૂલક વિજ્ઞાન', 'POSDCORB', 'હાયરાર્કી').
